# BlinkIT Grocery Sales Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes BlinkIT grocery sales data using Python to understand product performance, outlet performance, sales patterns, and customer ratings. The goal is to generate actionable business insights that support better inventory planning and business decisions.

## Objectives
- Understand overall sales performance and customer ratings.
- Clean the dataset by checking missing values, duplicate records, and inconsistent categories.
- Analyze sales across product categories, outlet types, outlet sizes, and locations.
- Examine relationships between sales and product characteristics.
- Identify high-performing and low-performing products and outlets.
- Develop data-driven business recommendations.

## Dataset
- **Records:** 8,523
- **Columns:** 12
- **Cleaned dataset:** `BlinkIT_Cleaned_Data (1).xlsx`

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- NumPy
- Matplotlib
- Seaborn
- Google Colab / Jupyter Notebook

## Key Findings
- **Total sales:** 1,201,681.49
- **Average sales per record:** 140.99
- **Average customer rating:** 3.97/5
- **Highest-selling category:** Fruits and Vegetables
- **Highest-sales outlet type:** Supermarket Type1
- **Highest-sales location tier:** Tier 3
- **Highest-sales outlet size:** Medium
- **Top product identifier:** FDU12
- **Top outlet identifier:** OUT035

## Visualizations and Analysis
The notebook includes data-quality checks, summary statistics, category-wise sales comparisons, outlet comparisons, scatter plots, and high- and low-performing product and outlet comparisons.

## Business Recommendations
- Review inventory planning for high-demand product categories.
- Study successful outlets to identify practices that may be replicated.
- Investigate lower-performing outlets and products to understand possible causes.
- Monitor sales trends and customer ratings regularly.
- Test proposed changes before implementing them at scale.
